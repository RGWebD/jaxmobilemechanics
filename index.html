FAQ

Straight answers.

If your question is not here, call or text (904) 800-8923.

Do you have a shop I can drive to?

No. Jacksonville Mobile Mechanics is a mobile, service-area business. We come to the vehicle. There is no public shop.

Where do you come to?

Jacksonville neighborhoods including Arlington, Southside, Mandarin, Westside, Northside, Riverside, San Marco, and the Beaches, plus Orange Park, Fleming Island, Middleburg, Baldwin, Yulee, and St. Johns.

What are your hours?

Monday through Thursday: open 24 hours. Friday and Saturday: 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. Sunday: open 24 hours. Times are Eastern.

How much is a diagnostic?

The diagnostic fee is $100.

Are repair quotes free?

Yes. Quotes on repairs are free. What a repair costs depends on the vehicle and the work, and we quote it before the repair.

Do you warranty the work?

Yes. There is a 30-day warranty on work.

Who owns the company?

Jamie Thompson Sr. and Shawntrell Williams. Jacksonville Mobile Mechanics LLC is a Florida limited liability company established in 2024.

Can you help if the vehicle will not start?

Yes. Call or text (904) 800-8923. We come to the vehicle for no-start problems, so you can skip a tow to a shop.

Do you work on fleets?

Yes. Fleet service is available. Call and tell us what needs service and where the vehicles are parked.

How do I request a visit?

Call or text (904) 800-8923, or use the form on this page. Have the year, make, model, and the place the vehicle is parked. The form opens a text message to the business. You still send that text yourself.
